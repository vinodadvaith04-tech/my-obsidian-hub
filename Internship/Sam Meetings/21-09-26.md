For example, if you command −50 mV and the LJP is +15.6 mV, the true membrane potential is −65.6 mV. You just apply this shift to all data in your analysis.

**In hardware (baked in before recording):** On an Axopatch, apply a holding command of −15.6 mV in voltage-clamp mode, then re-zero the Pipette Offset potentiometer. Reset the holding command to 0.
