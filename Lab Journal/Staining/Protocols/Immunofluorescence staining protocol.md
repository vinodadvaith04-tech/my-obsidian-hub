Raw protocol for immunofluorescence staining of hippocampal slices (free-floating, well plate). Copy this note into your experiment note and fill in the antibodies, dilutions and dates there.

#### Day 1

- [ ] **Fixation**
	Fix the slices with 4% PFA (in PBS) for 30 min at RT.
	0.5-1 ml per well
	30 mins

- [ ] **Washing**
	Wash 3x with 1x PBS (at least 5 min each)
	3 x 5 mins
	15 mins
	During the 3rd wash, prepare the permeabilisation solution

- [ ] **Permeabilisation solution**
	0.5% TritonX-100 in PBS
	For 10 ml:
		10X PBS            1 ml
		TritonX-100        50 µl (cut the tip of the pipette tip to pipette Triton)
		MQ water           8950 µl
		First add PBS, then TritonX, then MQ.
	For 3 ml:
		10X PBS            0.3 ml
		TritonX-100        15 µl
		MQ water           2685 µl

- [ ] **Permeabilisation**
	Permeabilise each slice with 250 µl 0.5% TritonX-100 in PBS (24 well plate).
	15 mins

- [ ] **Washing**
	3 x 10 mins
	30 mins

- [ ] **Blocking solution**
	10% goat serum + 0.2% TritonX-100 in 1x PBS. Make it for N+1. Keep on ice.
	For 10 ml:
		10X PBS                       1 ml
		TritonX-100                   20 µl (cut the tip of the pipette tip)
		100% normal goat serum        1 ml
		MQ water                      7980 µl
		First add PBS, then TritonX, then MQ.

- [ ] **Blocking**
	Add 250 µl blocking solution per slice, at RT, on the shaker.
	1 h

- [ ] **Primary antibody preparation**
	Dilute the primary antibodies in blocking solution (10% goat serum + 0.2% TritonX-100). Make it for N+1.
	Antibodies and dilutions: fill in per experiment.

- [ ] **Primary antibody addition**
	Add the primary solution to the slices. Leave one slice as a negative control.
	Overnight at 4 °C

#### Day 2

- [ ] **Washing**
	Wash 3x with 1x PBS
	3 x 10 mins

- [ ] **Secondary antibody preparation**
	Dilute the secondary antibodies in blocking solution (10% goat serum + 0.2% TritonX-100).
	Antibodies and dilutions: fill in per experiment. (Previous run: 3000 µl total volume.)

- [ ] **Incubation with secondary antibodies**
	3-4 hours on the shaker, covered

- [ ] **Washing**
	Wash 3x with 1x PBS (at least 10 min each)
	3 x 10 mins
