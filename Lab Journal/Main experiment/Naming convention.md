**On the 6 well plate (incubation and treatment)**
Slice date, plate number, well number, slice number, treatment code. 
eg 3/11 #2   -    1,2,3,4.    -    1,2,.....,12.      -      1A1&2

**While patching**
ADV, S.No, Sex, DIV, T.Code 
eg. ADV_12_female_15_2B123_001.abf

**On the 24 well plate (fixing)**
Date of fixing, PFA or PBS, Serial number. 
eg
	18/12
	PFA
	16
or
	5/11
	PBS
	21

Serial number 
eg. 13

Serial number along with side
eg 12 left
