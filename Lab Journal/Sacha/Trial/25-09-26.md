### Patching protocol###
0. Cap check (between every manual check)
1. CC AHP 800pA Multiple duration 5 second interval (which interval current injection is the best)
2. CC AHP 2ms 800pA (use your respective current injection)
3. CC AHP 3ms 800pA
4. CC AHP 4ms 800pA
5. CC HCN channel -600 - 0 pA 100pA steps (HCN channel depolarization/repolarization) (doesnt work on gerda, might have to change)
6. CC Spike frequency 0-300pA 25 steps 
7. CC Spike frequency 0-300pA 10 steps
8. VC clamp free EPSCs/IPSCs check
